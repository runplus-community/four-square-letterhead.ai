# FSL — Maintenance & Stewardship Skill

Project: four-square-letterhead.ai maintenance kit. Skill edition: v1.9. Four-Square Template v1.9. Maintenance guidance; not an installed agent, permission gate or schedule.

**Documents govern. Skills maintain. Evidence verifies.** Apply FSL-v1.9-METHOD.md as the FSL v1.9 method, not a replacement for the project's adopted business or technical intent. This skill is a maintenance aid, not an authority to approve changes.

### Locate the agreement and decision authority

Read the completed FSL first, supporting documents, versions and adopted changes. Identify authoritative homes across one or several repositories and the compatible baseline. Name the steward, executor and authority only where relevant; one person may hold several responsibilities. Check who can approve the particular decision and its recorded scope. Authorship, access, silence or task assignment does not grant approval. Intent change, execution/spending and release acceptance need their respective authority.

### Inspect alignment; expose uncertainty

At adoption, material changes, handover or release, compare agreed intent with the work, operating practice and applicable evidence. Describe any drift as expected versus observed, with source, affected baseline/route, impact and decision owner. Separate suspicion from a confirmed violation. Uncertain intent, authority, compatibility or proof remains explicit; pause dependent actions/claims and continue safe unaffected work. Do not silently reconcile competing records or rewrite released documents.

### Propose the smallest sufficient resolution

Repair implementation within granted scope when intent is unchanged. Update a stale working summary from its source. Review draft intent directly; use appendix for explanation, errata for intent-preserving correction and adopted amendment for changed released intent. A proposal is not approval; a skill update does not adopt a contract. Recheck affected routes and close the finding with linked decision/evidence. A scoped review may correctly conclude no change is needed.

### Estimate, ask and stay within the approved budget

GitHub Actions are **OFF by default**, including FSL's releases. General development or publication instructions are not execution permission. Inspect triggers before writes; pause any write that could start unapproved work. Suggestion is not approval. Recommend zero external starts when local work suffices.

Otherwise estimate need, repositories/workflow, candidate, evidence, least permissions, starts, jobs/downstream work, reads/fetches, duration and cost. Recommend one start per rolling 60 minutes, a task total and expiry; two needs justification and approval. These numbers grant no permission. Count failed, cancelled and uncertain attempts, retries and downstream starts. A single start is not unlimited jobs or runner time. An all-calls cap includes reads; otherwise specify their cap separately.

Share usage across approved repositories/agents with one accountable dispatcher; stop if consumption is unknown. Record time, run/job identity, actual calls and result. Rate, task-total, expiry, retry and cost limits all apply. Concurrency alone is not an hourly quota. No tight polling or blind timeout retries. Pause and request an extension before exceeding a bound. Required blocked checks remain NOT YET PROVEN, not waived.

### Verify and leave the next person a usable record

Show R-01's full touchpoint list; keep actual point results and evidence outside the letterhead. A component or stub check is not integrated E2E proof. Assignment is not execution: verify the collaborator's receipt and result. Old evidence records its original scope; assess relevance before reuse. A known binding violation is NON-CONFORMANT; unknown proof is NOT YET PROVEN.

Leave a dated note with effective baseline, key routes, supported claims, unproven scope, drift, next decision and owner; link authoritative records. Missing ownership stays unassigned. Skills and MEMORY retain learning without becoming a competing contract. This kit neither changes remote settings nor installs a rate limiter, background executor or drift monitor.
